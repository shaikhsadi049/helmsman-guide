## A. Baseline
- The slot is busy most of the time. Median hold is 2.0 days (mean 3.6), so only 46 of 329 signals are taken.
- Quarterly R: +3.4, +0.5, +6.0, +4.2, +9.4, +3.3, -0.3 (2026Q3 is July only).
- There are 9 losses, all of -1R. Losing months are 2025-05, 2025-10, 2026-02, 2026-03 and 2026-07, each between -0.1R and -1.0R.
- Profit is concentrated. One trade in Jan 2026 made +10.4R, which is 39% of the total; Jan 2026 alone made 10.5R. The top 5 days account for 83% of the result.
- Signals were 100% long until Feb 2026 and 100% short from Mar 2026 on. The short phase made +2.1R.

## B. Exits (all 3,600 trend policies, run with greedy one-at-a-time)
Each row holds the other components fixed and shows the median across policies.

| Component | Finding |
|---|---|
| Stop quantile | k70 is the sweet spot. k50 has more R per signal but maxDD 4-8R and 10-13/19 positive months. k90 is smoother but only reaches 16-20R. |
| TP1 lock ("slot" part, same as half@f30 for S1) | This is the smoothness engine: every k70/k90 lock variant has maxDD 1.1R. With no lock, k70 be1 gives 43.9R at DD 2.0R, but k70 with no lock and no be1 gives DD 3.6R. |
| Breakeven be1 (+0.05R after 1R) | It is needed whenever there is no lock. On top of the lock it adds a small gain (+1-2R). |
| Trail | h4q80, h4q50 and none differ by at most 2R. ATR chandelier trails are bad: atr2 gives 7R, and atr4 gives 14R with 9/19 positive months. |
| Fixed TP (1R/2R/3R/f50) or ratchet | These lower sumR. 2R_k50 and q70k50 raise eq_R2 to about 0.95-0.97 and cut top5days to 30-60%, but cost 4-8R and/or add DD (2.2-2.8R). |
| Time stop ts_half | Neutral to negative at k70. |

- **Regime-adaptive exit:** I ran `online_best_policy` between lock (1248) and no-lock (1320) with 14 regime features (ATR ranks, ADX, ER, d1_ret48, ribbon, hour and others). It gave 27-48R at DD 2.0-2.7R, the same as the unconditioned choice of 43.0R at DD 2.0R. No regime feature gives a stable gain.
- **Larger candidate sets:** choosing among 7 policies, all k70 policies or all 3,600 policies is worse: DD 3-5R and eq_R2 0.67-0.86.
- **Blends:** splitting the position between lock and runner (`E2.py`) works in a neighbourhood. Lock half in {1200, 1208, 1240, 1248} combined with a runner half that has be1 and an h4q80 or no trail, at weights 0.3-0.7, gives 30-39R, DD 1.1-1.7R and ret/DD 21-28. Runners with an h4q50 trail or without be1 push DD to 1.8-3.6R.

## E. Recommendation and robustness
- The exit is the 50/50 split above. The entry filter is none. Every input is already in Assay (k70, f30 TP1, the H4 tw80 trail) plus a +1R breakeven trigger and two tickets.
- **Result:** +8.5R (+32%) at the same DD, eq_R2 and ulcer. ret/DD is 28.2 vs 23.9 and positive months are 15/19 vs 14/19.
- **Where the gain comes from:** almost all of it (+8.6R) comes from big trend months (2025-08, 2025-12 and 2026-01).
  - Without Jan 2026 the split still wins: 18.9R vs 16.0R.
  - Month by month it beats the baseline in only 11 of 19 months.
  - Positive weeks fall from 80% to 73%.
- **Read it as:** better return per unit of pain, not a smoother curve.
- **How fragile it is:** 38-48 trades and 9 losses, so every effect is fragile. I chose a fixed 50/50 split rather than a fitted rule to limit over-fit.
- **Out of scope:** a second concurrent S1 position. Blocked signals are positive on average (+0.37R), but they are correlated with the open trade.
