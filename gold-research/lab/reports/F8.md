- **Target and time:** f50 or f70 with hm=1.0 is best in every row. hm=0.5 cuts R by about 35%, and hm=2.0 is no better. The "mean" target is the worst.
- **BE and ratchet:** be1 and q70k50 make no difference here. The results are identical in most cells, because fade targets are hit before these rules trigger. Leave them off.
- **Stop:** k50 beats k70 in all 15 tp × hm cells, both in greedy sumR and ret/DD, and in per-signal mean R. Without the greedy one-position rule, PF is flat across stops (k50/k70/k90 with f50: 1.61/1.66/1.69). So a tighter measured stop rarely changes the outcome, but it gives more R per unit of risk. Ungreedy ret/DD is 8.8 for k50/f50 vs 7.2 for k70/f50.
- **Is the stop effect consistent?** Per-signal k50−k70 (f50) by quarter, in R per signal: -0.02, +0.01, +0.05, -0.08, +0.01, **+0.40, +0.18**. The sign is the same in 5/7 quarters, but most of the size comes from 2026Q2–Q3.

## C. Entry take/skip
- **Univariate:** |Spearman| < 0.11 for all features except the `*_atr_pct` ones. Those reach 0.21, but they only track time: the quarter-consistency of their Q5−Q1 spread is 0/7. The consistent-sign features are trend strength: h4_er10, h1_adx and h1_er30, where high values are worse in 6/7 quarters. Even so, no quintile is negative on average.
- **Requested regimes:**
  - vol60_vs_day (news-like 1-minute bursts): all quintiles are positive, from +0.15 to +0.31R.
  - Strong daily trend (d1_er10, d1_adx, d1_ret12): no negative quintile and no consistent sign. Low d1_adx is the weakest quintile.
  - Volatility expansion (atr_ratio on M15/H1/H4/D1, day_rng_atr): flat.
- **Causal online quintile rules** (14 features): skip an extreme quintile when its shrunk past mean is below 0. For the k50 exit, every rule except m15_rsi2 (+3.4R, likely noise) lowered sumR. Results with the baseline exit were mixed: h4_er10 gave +4R, and prev_rng_atr lowered DD but not R.
- **Online LightGBM:** 16 variants per exit (F8 alone or pooled with F9–F11 plus a slot indicator; compact or all features; regression or classification; 2 thresholds). Spearman(pred, R) on 2025+ was between -0.09 and +0.02, so there is no skill. All variants with the k50 exit lost R (28–52R vs 59R).

## E. Recommended spec
- **Entry:** unchanged.
- **Exit:** a shadow book of 3 policies: stop k50, k70 or k90 (MAE quantiles, minimum 0.3 ATR), each with a whole-position TP at f50 and a time exit at 360 min. Each month, use the stop with the highest mean R over resolved past signals (from 2024-06). If no shadow book is possible, use a fixed k50/f50/360m. That choice is in-sample, but it is the robust direction.
- **Costs:** the median stop is about $21 at k70 and $13 at k50, so the $0.34 cost is only 0.016R (k70) or 0.027R (k50). The spec survives 3× costs (PF 1.43). Avoid k50 with a 1R target: under 3× cost it falls from +28.2R to +14.1R.

**Caveats:**
- The gain over baseline is concentrated in 2026-05 and 2026-07 (+16R of the +19R).
- In 2025 the tighter stop is neutral.
- Greedy one-at-a-time results are path-dependent; treat differences under about 10R as noise.
