## A. Baseline diagnosis
- **Payoff shape:** 77% of trades are tiny winners at about +0.14R, because the stop is locked at +0.1R once the trade reaches the measured TP1 (f20). About 23% are full -1R losses. The profit comes from 3 runners held 240 h (+12.1R, +11.7R, +7.5R). Without them the baseline is negative.
- **Direction:** longs +29.7R, shorts -4.9R.
- **Losing months:** 2025-01 -5.0, 2025-05 -2.1, 2026-07 -4.4. The rest is flat noise.

Neighbourhood (IN-SAMPLE) for part=none + q90k50, across sq × be × ts: sumR 66-90, maxDD 6.2-10, eq R² 0.91-0.95.

**Causal adaptive exit.** In the 2024 warm-up the ratchet was flat or negative, so only "no partial" was knowable on 2025-01-01. Results of the selectors:
- `online_best_policy` on per-signal mean, all 3,600 policies: +48R, DD 13.2. It ignores smoothness.
- Same with regime buckets (h4_er30, atr ranks, ADX, hour, …): +40 to +81R, DD 10-17. Nothing beat the plain selector consistently.
- **Shadow-book selector** (simulate each candidate's own one-at-a-time stream, pick the best past sumR/ulcer, refit monthly) over 12 candidates (k50/k70 × part slot/none × rat none/q90k50/2R_k50): +76.8R, DD 8.5 without the filter. It picked k70/k50 no-partial for Jan-Apr 2025, then k50 + q90k50 from 2025-05 onward.

- **Online ML** (LightGBM, monthly refit, past-resolved only) was not robust:

  - Pooled with S1-S6 plus a slot id: 41-70R.
  - Only the 2025-selected "extension" set looked great (88.7R, DD 4.2), and that set was chosen in-sample, so it is rejected.

## E. Recommended causal spec
1. **Exit:** shadow-book adaptive exit. Each month, pick among 12 candidates (k50/k70 × partial slot/none × ratchet none/q90k50/2R_k50; no TP, trail h4q80) by sumR/ulcer of each candidate's simulated past one-at-a-time stream. Its practical static equivalent is: **k50 stop, no partial / no +0.1R lock, H4 q80 trail, profit ratchet q90k50** (once open profit reaches f90, keep 50% of it).
2. **Entry:** skip when H1 ADX(14) is above the 80th percentile of H1 ADX over past S7 signals.
3. **Live inputs:** the same measured k/f quantiles Assay already computes (f90 in addition to f20), H1 ADX, and a 12-candidate shadow book.

## Robustness and caveats
- **8 causal shadow-book variants** (candidate set × objective × expanding/9-month window), all with the filter: +62 to +90R, maxDD 6.2-12.6, PF 2.1-2.7, 12-13/19 months positive. Every variant beats the baseline on sumR and months positive. Only about half beat the baseline maxDD of 7.5.
- **Still a runner system:** 35% win rate. The top 5 trades make 58.7R of the 80.2R; 2025-08 and 2026-01 alone make 40R. Holds are long (median 14 h, 90th percentile 111 h).
- **Portfolio overlap:** monthly correlation with the S1-S6 baseline sum is 0.86, and S7 loses in the same months the book loses (2026-03, 2026-07).
  - Monthly-equity maxDD: book alone 8.2R; + rec S7 at 1x 12.3R; at 0.5x 10.2R; + baseline S7 at 1x 12.6R.
  - Total: book alone 222R; + rec S7 at 1x 302R; at 0.5x 262R.
  - That is why S7 "hurt" before. **Re-enable at 0.5x risk.**
