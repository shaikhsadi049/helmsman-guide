# S2 (trend, M15 20-bar breakout): deep analysis

Window: 2025-01-01 .. 2026-07-30 only. Book: one position at a time (lab greedy), 1R risk per trade, net of costs.
Scripts are in `work_S2/`: a_base, b_rank, b_greedy, b_marg, b_neigh, b_online*, c_uni, c_rules, c_ml, c_online_rules, d_diag, d_filters, e_*.

**Verdict: improves.** The improvement comes entirely from the **exit**. No entry filter helped.

## B. Exits

All 3600 trend policies were run through the greedy book (`greedy_all.pkl`). Median over the policies that share each component value:

| component | finding (greedy sumR / maxDD) |
|---|---|
| stop | **k50 43.9R** vs k70 26.2 vs k90 19.9. A tighter stop gives shorter trades, so more signals get through the busy slot. k50 is right. |
| partial | none 33.1 vs **slot 24.0** vs half@f30 23.8. Assay's partial/lock costs about 25-45R whenever the other components are good. |

## C. Entry take/skip

- **Univariate** (169 features, quintiles, per quarter): there is one consistent theme, **overextension**. h1_adx, h1_slope50, h1_ribbon, h4_rng20_atr, d1_ret3, m15_dist200 and h4_ret12 all have Spearman IC between -0.15 and -0.21 against R under the 3R/ratchet exits, with the same sign in 5-6 of 6 quarters. Example: h1_adx Q1 +1.09R vs Q5 -0.21R per signal (pol 1041).
- **But under the one-position book, skipping does not help.** Skipped extended signals free the slot for the next signal of the same impulse. Fixed in-sample thresholds change sumR by only ±10%.
- **Causal filters on top of the recommended exit** (sumR, base 67.9):

| filter | skip% | sumR | maxDD | months+ |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| online threshold h1_adx high | 22 | 64.9 | 3.6 | 14/19 |
| online threshold h4_rng20_atr / ext3 | 35 | 61.7 | 3.6 | 14/19 |
| online feature+threshold search over all 169 | 38 | 56.5 | 3.6 | 14/19 |
| online LightGBM S2-only, all features, pred<0 | 51 | 67.0 | 3.6 | 15/19 |
| online LightGBM pooled with S1,S3-S7 (+slot ids) | 35-45 | 48.7-52.0 | 3.6 | 14/18 |
| causal side rule (skip a direction whose past mean <0) | 14 | 62.0 | 4.4 | 12/16 |
| low d1_er30 / d1_adx / h4_atr_rank / d1_dist200 | 2-11 | 49.6-65.2 | 3.6-4.7 | 12-14 |

The ML information coefficient (IC) is 0.04-0.16, which is too weak to pay for the signals it skips. **Recommendation: no entry filter.**

## D. Losing and flat months

Losing months under the recommended spec are 2025-05 (-2.0), 2025-06 (-1.6), 2026-03 (-0.9), 2026-05 (-0.9) and 2026-07 (-3.4). Compared with good months, the signals in these months had:

- **weak daily trend**: d1_er30 0.14 vs 0.34, d1_adx 22 vs 29, d1_dist200 z = -1.26;
- **compressed volatility**: h4_atr_rank 0.27 vs 0.58, d1_bbw_rank 0.46 vs 0.67;
- **fewer signals**: 16-35 per month vs 60-110.

From 2026-03 onward, 69-100% of S2 signals are **shorts**. Across 165 short signals the mean is ≈0R/signal (+0.04..+0.09 with the baseline or 1083 exits, -0.07 with 1041), against +0.42..+0.58R for longs. In-sample, longs-only would give 65.4R. The causal side rule cannot learn this in time (12/16 months positive, 62.0R). It is worth watching but not worth a rule.

## E. Recommended causal spec vs baseline

- **Exit:** the shadow-book learner described in B.
  - Candidates: all 240 trend exits that use the k50 stop and the h4q80 trail.
  - Each month, for each candidate, run a greedy simulation on past known S2 signals with an expanding window. Score = sumR / max(maxDD, 1R).
  - Take the top 8 singles and also score every ½/½ blend of two of them (½ lot per leg; the slot stays busy until both legs close). Trade the best single or blend.
  - If a learner is not wanted, a static equivalent is to split the lot: leg A = pol 1083, leg B = pol 1085 (k50, 3R TP, no partial, BE1 after 1R, ratchet q90k50 / q70k50, ts_half).
- **Entry:** none. Take every S2 signal while the slot is free.
