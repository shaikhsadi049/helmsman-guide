- **Stop:** k70 is clearly the best stop level. k50 roughly doubles the drawdown, and k90 halves the profit.
- **Target:** with k70, both 1R and "mean" beat f50 at every horizon.
- **Causal adaptive exit:** `lab.online_best_policy` chose among the 5 targets with k70 and hm1. Using only warm-up outcomes it picked "mean" in the first month, and kept it in every month with F10 signals (all 111 signals), giving +16.2R. With pooled F8-F11 history the result is +15.6R.
- **Adding the horizon to the candidate set hurts.** With 15 candidates the result is +9.7R, and with all 45 it is +8.3R. Regime-bucketed versions (11 regime features × 2-3 buckets × 3 candidate sets × 2 pools) ranged from +1.7R to +23.0R. The best was the h1_z20 tercile with pooled data, but that is one pick out of 138 configurations, so it is not recommended.
- **Caveat:** the "mean" target does **not** help the sibling fades F8 and F9 (there f50 is better), so this result is specific to F10.

## C. Entry take/skip
- **Univariate:** the top features form one family, "the fade has already started bouncing": signed m15_ret24/ret12, m5_rsi2, m15/m5 dist20 and m5_streak. IC is -0.22 to -0.29, with the same sign in 6-7 of 7 quarters, and the bottom quintile has mean R around +0.4 to +0.57. The largest |IC| (0.29) is at permutation-noise level, though (the null max over 169 features is about 0.27).
- **Pair:** m5_rsi2 > median and m15_ret12 > median gives mean R -0.36 vs +0.38 for the rest, and is worse in 7 of 7 quarters.

## D. Losing months
The losing months are 2025-04, 2025-11, 2026-01, 2026-02 and 2026-04: 23 trades, -3.5R. They are high-volatility expansion months. Compared with good months, d1/h4/h1/m15 atr_pct is +1.3 to +1.4 SD and d1_bbw_rank is +1.3 SD, and M5/M15 range efficiency is lower. The stretched move keeps going instead of reverting. Volatility skip-rules remove winners too: a gated skip on high d1_atr_pct gives +10.4R and one on high h1_atr_pct gives +12.6R.

## E. Recommended spec
- **Exit:** a monthly online best-target choice between {f30, f50, f70, 1R, mean}, with a k70 stop (min 0.3) and a 480-minute time exit, learned from a shadow book of past resolved F10 signals. Today it means: **take the whole position when the M30 close returns to the 20-bar mean.**
- **Entry:** unchanged (no filter).
- **Live inputs:** M30 SMA20, k70, ATR, and the shadow book.
- **Robustness:** the neighbourhood (k70 with mean or 1R at every horizon) is 13-16R, against 10.3R for the baseline. With only 111 signals, the gain is about 0.07R per trade.
