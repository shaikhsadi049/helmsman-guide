# S5 (trend, M5 EMA30 dip-and-reclaim): research report

**Verdict: no robust improvement.** Keep today's entry and exit. The smoothest exit found (a 3R cap plus the q70k50 ratchet) holds up across nearby settings, but it was chosen on 2025+ data and no causal selector picks it. The variant is listed below as an optional, in-sample choice.

All numbers cover 2025-01..2026-07 and use the one-position-at-a-time greedy rule. Scripts are in `work_S5/`.

## C. Entry take/skip
- **Univariate results under both exits:** "over-extended trend = worse" is consistent across quarters. Top-quintile vs bottom-quintile mean R and IC by feature:
  - d1_ret3: IC -0.18, 5/6 quarters
  - h4_di: IC -0.16
  - h4_adx: IC -0.15
  - h1_ribbon: IC -0.14, 5/6 quarters
  - h4_rng20_atr, d1_z20, h4_slope50: 5-6/6 quarters
  - f50: high recent favourable move is worse
- **Online quintile-skip rules** (thresholds learned monthly from past-known outcomes, one rule per feature, about 170 features): only 6% (exit 972) and 10% (baseline) of the rules beat the unfiltered ret/DD, which is noise level. Rules built on the extension features cut sumR to 37-45R.
- **Online LightGBM**, run on S5 alone and pooled with S1-S7 using slot indicators, with regression and classification targets and all/extension/HTF feature subsets. Signal-level IC reached 0.17 (pooled classifier, extension features). At trade level the best result was 110 trades, 56.6R, maxDD 3.1, 17/19 months, eq_R2 0.957, against 60.1R and eq_R2 0.982 unfiltered. Every other variant was worse.
- **Why filters fail here:** skipping one signal of a cluster usually just moves the entry to the next signal of the same episode, and the greedy path changes. Signal-level predictability does not carry through to the equity curve.

## E. Recommendation
- **Recommended (causal): the baseline, unchanged.** Exit k50 / slot partial at f50 with a +0.25R lock / h4q50 runner. No entry filter. Metrics are as in section A.
- **Optional, in-sample only:** add a 3R cap on the runner and the measured q70k50 profit ratchet (policy 972).
  - It has the same drawdown as baseline and +6R more.
  - Top-5-day concentration falls from 76% to 32%, and eq_R2 rises from 0.946 to 0.982.
  - The cost is the large trend runs (Jan-26).
  - It is structurally sensible and holds up across nearby settings. It is not causally validated, so it should be watched in the shadow book before adoption.
- **Do not add a direction filter.** Shorts made +6.4R under 972 in 2026Q2.
