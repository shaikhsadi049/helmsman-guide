## A. Baseline
- 83% winners but median trade +0.14R: TP1 (f20 ≈ 2.3 ATR) is only ~0.3R against a k70 stop (≈8 ATR), and the stop then sits at +0.25R, so most trades end as +0.1..+0.2R scratches while losers are a full -1R.
- The profit is one runner: 2026-01 = 8.7R of 15R (one day = 8.0R); top-5 days = 114% of the profit. Losing quarter 2025Q2 (-1.5R) and 2026Q3 (-1.7R); 7/19 months negative.

- The baseline is in the bottom tail of all 3600 policies. The Assay partial/lock is the single most harmful component.
- Continuous ATR trails produce the smoothest curves (eq_R2 0.95-0.98, DD 1-3R, top-5 days 30-55%); H4 trails and no-trail give more R per trade but lumpy curves (top-5 days 70-97%).
- Neighbourhood of the recommendation: every k70 / no-partial / atr4 variant (5 TPs × be on/off) = 27.0-29.6R, DD 2.1-2.2R, 15/19 months; k50 = 33.8-39.8R, DD 2.8-3.7R; k90 = 22.5-24.0R, DD 1.9-2.2R. The recommended exit beats baseline in 5 of 6 complete quarters (Q 2025Q1..2026Q2: 2.6/5.1/4.8/4.7/6.1/4.1 vs 3.3/-1.5/3.2/1.4/7.3/3.2).
- Causal adaptive exits:
  - `online_best_policy` over 11 archetypes, global or by regime (16 regime features × 2-3 bins): 28-51R, DD 3.1-6.6R, no regime consistently better — noise.
  - Per-signal mean/Sharpe/Sortino argmax over 3600 policies: 19-28R, DD 4.8-7.8R (ignores overlap, picks long holds).
  - Shadow-book (re-simulate the one-at-a-time book of each of 1757 distinct policies on resolved history, pick monthly): 38.8-43.3R, DD 5.5R, 12/19 months, under every criterion (sum, sum/DD, sum×R², ulcer). It converged to k50/no partial/be1/h4q50/ts_half. Causal learning confirms "no lock, k50, let it run" but optimises R, not smoothness.

## C. Entry take/skip (exit k50/be1/atr4; same conclusions under k70 atr4)
- Univariate (IC, quarters with the same sign): prev_rng_atr -0.23 (5/6), d1_ret3 -0.22 (5/6), h4_rng20_atr -0.20 (6/6), h1_adx -0.19 (6/6), h4_ret24 -0.19 (6/6), h4_rsi14 -0.18 (6/6). All say **overextended entries are weaker** (top quintile ≈ -0.1R vs +0.27R overall).
- But in the one-at-a-time book skipping them gains nothing: a skipped signal is replaced by the next one. Results (sumR / DD): take all 39.3/2.8; causal q80 threshold on h1_adx 39.1/2.9, h4_rsi14 39.6/2.9, h4_rng20_atr 37.1/3.0, prev_rng_atr 33.2/2.1; online rule learned over all 169 features 31.1/3.8.
- Online LightGBM: S4-only all features 37.3/2.2 (skips 61%), 13 features 19.2/3.3, pooled with trend siblings (+slot id) 24.3/2.8, classifier 25.8/3.6. Equity-curve filter (last 10-40 resolved signals) 7.6-25.0R. **No ML filter is worth it.**
- The only robust effect: **H4 6-EMA stack not aligned** (h4_stack != +1, 21 signals): mean R -0.41/-0.34/-0.47/-0.13/+0.47/-0.59 by quarter. A causal skip (on while past resolved such signals have mean < 0; active 19/19 months) improves every non-runner exit, e.g. k70 atr4 27.0R/DD 2.1 → 29.2R/DD 1.1, k50 atr4 39.3/2.8 → 41.5/2.4.

## D. Losing / flat months
Bad months under the new exit: 2025-02, 2025-05, 2026-03, 2026-07 (2026-04/05 flat). Compared with good months (standardised difference of signal features): d1_dist200 -1.04 sd, dxy_trend -0.77 (dollar moving against the trade), d1_rng20_atr -0.64, d1_adx -0.53, d1_atr_rank +0.48. So the losses come from **D1 trend exhaustion or regime flips in high daily volatility**: 2025-05 (D1 er10 0.05, post-April spike), 2026-03 (long→short flip; every signal since 2026-04 is short). Shorts are weaker per signal (+0.04R vs +0.31R longs) but still net +3.4R in the book. None of these D1 features gave a usable causal filter (see C).

## E. Recommended spec (live)
1. Entry unchanged, except skip when the H4 6-EMA stack is not aligned with the trade (causal switch as above).
2. Stop: k70 measured stop (unchanged).
3. No partial, **no TP1 lock**, no fixed TP.
4. Trail: chandelier at 4 × M30 ATR(14) from the best price since entry (continuous, on every bar).
5. Optional higher-return variant: k50 stop + BE (+0.05R after 1R) + same trail: +41.5R, DD 2.4R.

Live inputs: M30 ATR(14), the k70 MAE quantile (already in Assay), H4 EMA30/35/40/45/50/60, and a shadow book of past S4 signals for the H4 switch.

Caveat: the atr4 family is a fixed choice made with full-window knowledge (labelled in-sample). Its neighbourhood is uniformly good and the honest causal shadow-book counterpart (+40.4R, DD 4.3R) also clearly beats the baseline, so the direction of the change is causally supported. How smooth the result is depends on choosing the continuous trail.
