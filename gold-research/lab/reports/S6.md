## C. Entry take/skip (under the recommended exit)
- **Univariate:** atr_pct features show the highest IC, but they are nonstationary price proxies, so they were excluded. The effects that are consistent across quarters all say that an extended H4 trend hurts: h4_slope50, h4_rng20_atr and h4_ret24 have a negative top-minus-bottom spread in 6/6 quarters, and h4_adx and d1_z20 in 5/6.
- **Online quintile rules** (13 features, S6 or pooled): none raises sumR above 90.2 except within noise. The best (h1_adx, h4_ret12, d1_ret3) lift PF to about 3.1 but end at 80-87R.
- **Online LightGBM** (all, h4d1 and consistent-15 features; S6 alone or pooled with S3 plus a slot flag; regression on clipped R or win classification): IC is 0.07-0.16. Skipping pred<0 lifts PF to 3.2-3.8 and some variants reach 17-18/19 months, but sumR falls to 52-89R. **No robust gain, so the recommendation is no filter.**

## D. Losing and flat months (2025-02, 2026-03, 2026-05, 2026-07)
In the weak months:
- Price sat much closer to the D1 EMA200 (d1_dist200 3.5 vs 7.2 ATR).
- The D1 range and volatility rank were lower, and H4 slope and ribbon were weaker.
- DXY trend was adverse (-0.19 vs +0.47).
- Short-TF ATR% was higher.

In short, these were consolidating or correcting gold markets with no D1 impulse. Mean R per signal in those months was below 0. A causal d1_dist200 quintile skip gives 92.3R with maxDD 3.0, but this rule was found post-hoc. It should be monitored, not adopted.
