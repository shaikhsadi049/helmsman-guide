## C. Entry take/skip
- **Univariate:** the top effects are on the daily timeframe (d1_streak, d1_rsi2, d1_z20, d1_ret1; |IC| 0.27-0.32), but they hold in only 2-4 of 7 quarters, so they are not consistent.
- **Online LightGBM:** 16 configurations (own or pooled F8-F11 with a slot indicator; all, momentum, regime and momentum+regime features; regression or classification). Out-of-sample IC was -0.14 to +0.06, and **every configuration did worse than baseline** (-0.3 to +5.2R).
- **Online threshold rules:** -1.1 to +8.6R, with the chosen feature changing from month to month.
- **h4_er30 (H4 efficiency ratio, 30 bars):**
  - The low tercile (choppy H4) is bad: tercile means are -0.25, +0.18 and +0.34R.
  - The same direction shows up in F10 (worse in 5 of 6 quarters) and in F9 2025+, but not in F8.
  - The causal gate (threshold = past 25th percentile; skip only while past resolved F11 signals in that tail have a negative shrunk mean) was **on in all 17 months, using warm-up data alone**.
  - The result is robust to the cut: q20/25/33/40 give +9.6/+10.5/+11.7/+10.1R. It also helps the f30 exit (+11.4R, PF 1.98, DD 2.6, top-5 days 56%).
  - **Selection check:** the same gated rule was run for all 169 features × 2 tails. The median result was +5.6R and the 5-95% range was +2.0 to +7.5R. h4_er30-low ranked 2nd of 336, so selection bias cannot be ruled out.
  - It is therefore listed as a shadow candidate only, not adopted.

## D. Losing months
The losing months are 2025-01, 2025-02, 2025-04, 2026-03 and 2026-07: 22 trades, -8.2R. In those months daily volatility rank is low (d1_atr_rank -1.1 SD), and so are the D1/H4 variance ratio (-0.6 to -0.8 SD) and H4 atr_rank. In quiet, directionless markets the false break has no follow-through back across the range. Two thirds of the losses are full stops: 17 of 70 trades lost at least 0.8R. Gating on volatility did not help causally (+2.5 to +6.4R).

## E. Recommendation
- Keep the baseline exit (k70 stop min 0.3, f50 target, 720-minute time exit) and take all signals.
- Run the h4_er30 bottom-quartile gate, and optionally the f30 target, in the EA's shadow book.
- Adopt either one only if it keeps beating the baseline on signals after 2026-07.
- **Overall:** F11 is marginal (PF 1.28; the top 5 days are 111% of the profit). It is not bad enough to drop, but it should run at low weight.
