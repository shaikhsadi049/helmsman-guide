# S3 (trend, M3 RSI(2) pullback): analysis report

Window: 2025-01-01 .. 2026-07-30 only. All numbers come from the one-position greedy `run_slot`, in R after costs. Scripts: `work_S3/`.

## Verdict: **improves, but only the exit and only modestly.** Add a 3R hard target on the runner. Keep the entry as it is.
The change moves profit away from a single month and spreads it over the year: top-5-days share drops from 73% to 28%, equity R² rises from 0.921 to 0.977, monthly Sharpe rises from 0.55 to 0.88, and the total without Jan-2026 rises from 24.5R to 42.3R. It costs a little drawdown (2.7R to 3.5R) and PF (2.38 to 1.92).
None of the causal entry filters, rules or ML, beat the unfiltered entry robustly.

What the component tests show:
1. **The target matters most.** A 3R cap on the runner cuts mean hold from 20 h to 8 h, adds 80 trades, and turns R per day in market from 0.33 to 0.54. It is robust in its neighbourhood: k70/3R, k90/2R, k70/3R+q90k50, k70/3R+ts_half and k70/half@f30/3R all give 15–17/19 months, eq_R2 0.97–0.98 and top5d 26–29%.
2. **The trail barely matters once a cap is in place.** With 3R, h4q50, h4q80 and no trail give identical results. A tight continuous ATR trail (atr2) is ruinous: −4R over 1,008 trades.
3. **Stop:** k70 is the sweet spot. k50 earns more R but doubles to triples drawdown and concentration. k90 is smooth but earns less.
4. Dropping the partial (2288: k70/3R/no partial/be1) gives +84R with DD 5.8, 15/19 months and ulcer 1.97. That is the highest causal return, but it is less smooth.

**Regime-dependent exits.** I tested an online regime chooser (`online_best_policy` logic, with terciles from past quantiles) over 14 regime features: ATR ranks, ER, ADX, day range, hour, d1_ret24, BBW and others. The candidate sets were {base, 3R}, {k70 slot × TP}, 6 mixed policies, and the 3R family, each under a mean-R objective and an R-per-time-in-market objective. **No regime split beat the best unconditioned choice.** Most regime choosers collapse to one policy.

The unrestricted causal search over 3,600 policies overfits. It hops between k50 runner policies and is **less smooth than the baseline**. Any honest causal chooser restricted to the Assay structure (slot partial plus a cap) ends up between 2R and 3R. That gives 38–53R, 15–17/19 months and top5d 28–32%. The 3R cap is the best of that range, and two chooser variants pick it causally.

## C. Entry take/skip (exit 2168 and baseline)
- **Univariate quintiles (169 features).** The most consistent effects under 2168 are low intraday activity:
  - m15_vr10 Q1 mean +0.01 vs Q5 +0.35 (same sign in 6/7 quarters)
  - day_rng_atr Q1 +0.02 vs Q5 +0.35 (5/6)
  - m15_er30 (6/7), day_pos (6/7)

  Under the baseline exit, the high side of h1_adx/h4_adx/h4_rng20 looks worst. That signal is noisy because a few runners dominate it.
- **Pairs (3×3 terciles of the top 25).** Worst cell: day_rng_atr low & d1_vol_ratio high, −0.23 R per signal, negative in 4/4 quarters. As a causal rule it scores 49.6R and 15/19 months, worse than no filter.
- **Online thresholds** (the bottom or top 20%/33% bucket is skipped only if its past mean R < 0). Tested for 20 features and both exits. None improved sumR and smoothness together. The best is h1_adx top-20% skip: 48.0R, 18/19 months, ulcer 1.09, eq_R2 0.983, which is −4.6R. That is marginal. The month-by-month automatic "best single cut" scored 39.3R.
- **Online LightGBM** (S3 only, and pooled with S1–S7 using a slot indicator; regression and win/loss classification). IC is only 0.05–0.13. Skipping when pred < 0 gives 46–50R vs 52.6R. Against the baseline exit it scores 28–39R vs 40.9R. **No gain.**
- **Shadow-equity filter** (skip when the mean of the last N known shadow outcomes < 0): clearly harmful, 23–48R with worse DD. Losses cluster less than the gains do.
- **Skipping shorts causally** helps only the baseline exit: PF 3.32 and +42.8R, but 5 months have no trades and eq_R2 falls to 0.88. Under the 3R exit shorts are net positive (+5.1R), and skipping them hurts (42.9R).

## D. Losing and flat months
Signal-level 2168 means are ≤ 0.05 in 2025-01/02/07/08/09/12 and 2026-03/04/07. Compared with good months, the signals in those months show:
- **lower D1/H4 volatility and expansion**: d1_atr_rank −0.98σ, h4_atr_pct −0.79σ, h4_vr10 −0.73σ (same side in 9/9 bad months), d1_atr_ratio, BBW
- **a weaker D1 trend**: d1_ribbon −0.70σ, d1_adx −0.44σ
- **stronger short-term M15/H1 momentum**: h1_ret3/6, m15_er30 and m15_ret24 at +0.2–0.3σ, same side in 9/9

In short, S3 loses when a quiet, range-bound daily market produces intraday trends that do not follow through. These conditions persist for weeks, so a causal learner only sees them after the fact. A past-quantile rule on d1_atr_rank, d1_ribbon or h4_vr10 never fired on the 2168 exit, because the past low bucket was not negative. A learner cannot use this pattern yet, though it explains the flat months.

## E. Recommended causal spec
- **Exit:** policy 2168 = `{sq: k70, tp: 3R, part: slot (half at measured TP1 f20, lock +0.1R), be: none, trail: h4q50 (kept, rarely binds), rat: none, ts: none}`. This is the baseline plus a 3R hard target on the runner. Live, it can be run as a shadow book that compares base and 3R on R per minute in market. That chooser picked 3R in every month from warm-up data.
- **Entry filter:** none. Take every signal when the slot is free.

- **Neighbourhood holds:** k90/2R, k70/3R+q90k50 and k70/3R+ts_half are all 15–17/19 months with top5d 26–29%.
