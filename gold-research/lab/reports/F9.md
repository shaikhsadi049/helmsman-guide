## A. Baseline
- There were 158 F9 signals in 2025+ (and 82 warm-up signals in 2024). With one position at a time, 89 were traded. Signals cluster on consecutive H1 bars.
- Monthly R: losses in 2025-01 (-0.4), 2025-04 (-0.5) and **2026-07 (-3.8R, 11 trades, 27% win)**. 2025-02, 2025-07 and 2026-06 were flat. There were no signals in 2025-09. 2026-07 erased about a third of the total.
- By quarter: +1.4, +3.4, +0.5, +2.1, +3.9, +5.4, -3.8.
- Sells (+0.22R/signal) beat buys (+0.09) under the baseline exit. The 2026Q3 buys averaged -0.40R.

## C. Entry take/skip
- Univariate, F9 2025+, using the k70/f70/be1/hm2 exit (quartile means, low to high):
  - `h1_bbw_rank` (squeeze): -0.29 / 0.31 / 0.37 / 0.62. Fading a 2-SD close out of a squeeze loses.
  - `h1_dist20` (stretch in ATR): 0.54 / 0.49 / 0.24 / -0.27. A shallow stretch loses.
  - `d1_stack` signed: against the trade -0.11 (n=73) vs with it +0.53 (n=71), worse in 5/7 quarters.
  - `m5_atr_pct`: -0.12 → +0.57.
- Pooled over the fade slots, these effects mostly shrink (IC below 0.19). h1_bbw_rank has no effect on F8 or F10.
- Causal filters that I tested, none of which beat take-all:

| filter | result |
|---|---|
| online bin rules, 17 features × 2-4 bins, F9 history | 2.5-13.5R, fewer trades, never above baseline + adaptive exit |
| online LightGBM, F9 only or pooled F8-F11 with slot one-hot, all 169 features, regression and classification | 8-16.5R (IC 0.02-0.20) vs 15.1R without a filter. Best: pooled classifier at threshold 0.5 gave 16.5R, DD 2.2R, but 12/18 months, and its neighbours (threshold 0.4: 14.1R; regression: 13.0R) do not confirm it. |

- **Why d1_stack cannot be learned causally:** in the cumulative history, counter-daily-trend fades were the *best* group at every month start through 2026-07 (+0.11..+0.17R vs -0.32..+0.13R for with-trend fades). Rolling-window versions:
  - 90-day window, F9 only: 14.9R, DD 2.0
  - 180- and 270-day windows: 6-17R
  - pooled: 8-17R

  The 90-day result is not confirmed by its neighbours, so I treat it as a hypothesis to monitor.
- The warm-up mean R was negative (-0.10), so "skip if predicted < 0" learners start out over-skipping.

## D. Losing / flat months (2025-01, 02, 04; 2026-03, 07) vs good months
- Losing months had more buys (66% vs 53%) and more counter-daily-trend entries (mean d1_stack -0.09 vs +0.03, d1_slope50 -0.09 vs +0.04). They also had lower H4 ATR rank (0.34 vs 0.51), higher H1 ADX (27.3 vs 23.9) and a falling DXY trend.
- 2026-07: every buy signal was against a bearish daily stack, several came out of H1 squeezes (bbw rank 0.02-0.2), and they lost about -1R each. Sells that month (with the daily trend) won.
- Gold was trending down on the daily chart, so buying H1 dips failed.

## E. Recommendation
- **Exit:** a shadow book. Simulate all 180 fade exit variants (stop k50/70/90 × target f30/f50/f70/1R/mean × be1 × ratchet × horizon ×0.5/1/2) on every F9 signal's 1m path. At each month start, use the variant with the best mean R over F9 signals that have already resolved.
- **Fixed fallback** if the shadow book is not built: k70 stop, f70 target, be1, horizon 1440 min. This is in-sample selected but neighbourhood-robust.
- **Entry:** unchanged (take all).
- **Monitor** with-daily-trend vs counter-trend fades on a rolling 90-day window. Promote it to a filter only if it stays positive out of sample.
- **Result:** +15.1R vs +12.8R, maxDD 3.5R vs 5.5R, ulcer 1.18 vs 1.45, PF 1.70 vs 1.45. The R gain is within noise (about 1σ); the drawdown cut is the solid part.
